The game

Picking a genre gives the AI real technical scaffolding for that kind of game (physics, spawning, grid logic) instead of guessing — usually the single biggest jump in quality.

Upload short clips or record your voice — they're baked directly into the game file and never sent anywhere, including to the AI provider. Give each one a short name; the AI uses the name to decide when to play it (e.g. "jump", "win", "taunt"). Keep clips short — they're embedded as-is.

Be specific about controls and the win condition if you have one in mind — the AI follows what you give it. Expect simple, playable mechanics with shapes and emoji for characters, not illustrated art.
