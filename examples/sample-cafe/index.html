What We're About

The local living room you've been searching for

We started Fernway with a simple idea: that a café should feel like your best friend's front porch. A place where conversations flow, time slows down, and everyone is welcome.

🌱

Small-Batch Coffee

We work directly with sustainable family farms to source exceptional beans, roasting them right down the street in microlots.

Porch Energy

Daily Pastries
