Our Story

Small-batch coffee, big front porch energy

Rooted in East Nashville

Fernway Coffee was born out of a simple belief: that a neighborhood café should feel like an extension of your own living room. Located right at 104 Fernway Lane, we set out to build a space where time slows down just a little bit.

Whether you’re grabbing a quick cortado on your morning commute or settling in with a book for the afternoon, our doors are open to everyone who makes East Nashville so vibrant.

Small-Batch, Crafted with Care

We partner closely with independent local roasters who share our obsession with quality, ethics, and sustainability. Every single batch of beans is sourced with intention, roasted to highlight its unique origin notes, and brewed with precision.

From our house syrups made from scratch to our rotating single-origin pours, we treat every cup as a craft—never rushed, always exceptional.

Big Front Porch Energy

You won't find corporate stiffness here. Pull up a chair on our porch, wave to the neighbors walking their dogs, and enjoy a warm cup crafted by people who genuinely love what they do.
