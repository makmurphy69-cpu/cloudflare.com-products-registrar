Our Beginnings

Crafted for the quiet, slow moments.

We believe that a candle should be more than home fragrance. It should be a gentle invitation to pause, unwind, and dwell in the present.

Ember & Oak was born out of a desire to find quiet amidst the constant rush of modern life. Rooted in the therapeutic rhythm of small-batch crafting, each of our candles is poured by hand with undivided focus, clean ingredients, and a dedication to unmatched sensory quality.

The Philosophy

Clean materials. Intentional design.

What goes into your home matters deeply to us. Standard commercial candles are often loaded with synthetic paraffins and harsh chemical additives. We set out to create an alternative that is kind to your lungs and beautiful to your spaces.

Every single Ember & Oak candle is formulated using 100% natural soy wax sourced sustainably from domestic farmers. We pair our wax with botanical fragrance oils and clean crackling wooden wicks to produce a steady, toxic-free burn that gently warms your home without overwhelming it.

Our Method

Made by Hand in Small Batches

We refuse to compromise quality for scale. That is why our process remains strictly manual, slow, and intentional from start to finish.

Step 01

The Scent Profile

We meticulously test and blend complex fragrance profiles inspired by nature—woods, dry spices, earthy resins, and fresh botanicals—to recreate the nostalgic aroma of a forest retreat.

Step 02

The Slow Pour

Wax is melted to its precise temperature and blended with oils, then poured by hand into our signature amber glass vessels. Hand-pouring reduces air bubbles and ensures an even distribution.

Step 03

The Cure & Trim

Each batch is allowed to cure slowly in a temperature-controlled environment for two weeks. This curing process binds the oils to the wax, ensuring maximum hot scent throw.

A Note From Our Founder

"I wanted to build a sanctuary within the home."

My journey into candlemaking began in a tiny studio apartment during a chaotic season of life. Every evening, my ritual was to light a candle and sit with a book. That simple flicker of warm light and grounding earthy scent changed the energy of the entire space. It became my sanctuary.

I crafted Ember & Oak to share that sanctuary with you. I hope our candles inspire you to dim the harsh overhead lights, silence the screens, and enjoy a slow, quiet evening. You’ve earned it.

Claire Thorne
