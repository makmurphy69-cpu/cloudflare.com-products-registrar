  5. Which condition has these signs? “Chest pain on exertion (angina), breathlessness.”

    4. Heart attack

Answer key

  1. C) Heart attack — Heart attack (Heart) — signs: Chest pain or pressure spreading to the arm, jaw or back, breathlessness, sweating, nausea.
  2. C) Heart — Heart failure: The heart can’t pump as well as it should.
  3. C) Lungs — Heart + Lungs: Sends blood through the lungs to collect oxygen.
  4. D) Heart failure — Heart failure (Heart) — signs: Breathlessness, swollen ankles, tiredness.
  5. C) Coronary heart disease — Coronary heart disease (Heart) — treatment: Lifestyle changes plus medicines (statins, aspirin, blood pressure tablets); stents or bypass surgery when needed.
