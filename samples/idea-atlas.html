Answer key

  1. B) Fascism — Adolf Hitler — Nazi dictator responsible for the Holocaust (key work: Mein Kampf).
  2. C) Social democracy — Eduard Bernstein — Revisionism: socialism through reform (key work: Evolutionary Socialism).
  3. C) Keynesian economics — John Maynard Keynes — Aggregate demand; government stabilisation (key work: The General Theory).
  4. A) Mogobe Ramose — Mogobe Ramose (contemporary), a key figure of Ubuntu philosophy: Systematic philosophy of ubuntu.
  5. A) René Descartes — René Descartes (1596–1650), a key figure of Rationalism: “Cogito, ergo sum”; methodical doubt.
  6. C) Zhuangzi — Zhuangzi (c. 369–286 BCE), a key figure of Daoism (Taoism): Explored freedom, relativity of viewpoints and spontaneity through stories.
  7. A) Pragmatism — Pragmatism: An American philosophy that judges ideas by their practical consequences. A belief is true if it works — if acting on it succeeds in experience. Pragmatists applied this to science, religion, education and democracy.
  8. A) 1883 — This happened in 1883, in the history of Existentialism.
  9. A) Classical economics & free-market capitalism — Libertarianism drew on Liberalism, Classical economics & free-market capitalism.
  10. B) Existentialism — Existentialism: A philosophy focused on individual existence, freedom and choice. Existentialists argue that life has no pre-given meaning: we create meaning through our choices and must take responsibility for them, even in the face of anxiety and absurdity.
